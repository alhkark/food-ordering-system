Food ordering services are installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Cluster DNS (same namespace):
  order-service:8181
  payment-service:8182
  restaurant-service:8183
  customer-service:8184

From another namespace:
  order-service.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:8181
  payment-service.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:8182
  restaurant-service.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:8183
  customer-service.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:8184

Images must already exist locally (imagePullPolicy: IfNotPresent), for example:
  ./gradlew :order-service:order-container:bootBuildImage
  ./gradlew :payment-service:payment-container:bootBuildImage
  ./gradlew :restaurant-service:restaurant-container:bootBuildImage
  ./gradlew :customer-service:customer-container:bootBuildImage

Postgres, Kafka, and Schema Registry are not part of this chart. Point
datasource.url, kafka.bootstrapServers, and kafka.schemaRegistryUrl at
Services in this cluster (same namespace: postgres, broker-1, schema-registry).
